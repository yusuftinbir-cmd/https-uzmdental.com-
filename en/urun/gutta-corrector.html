Product Details & Technical Specifications

UZM Dental Gutta Percha Point Corrector is a precision measuring and cutting apparatus developed to rapidly verify the tip diameter of gutta-percha points and trim them to the desired ISO size when required.

When a matching gutta-percha size is unavailable chairside, the tip of a smaller cone can be trimmed in a controlled manner to match the target ISO diameter, simplifying master cone selection prior to obturation.

Key Features ⇊
ISO-Marked Calibration

Measuring holes marked according to ISO standard sizes for reliable tip verification.

Stainless-Steel Body

Durable, long-lasting construction with an easy-to-clean surface.

Fast Master Cone Fit

Helps clinicians reach the required ISO apical size quickly at chairside.

Economical Use

Reduces the need to stock every individual ISO size separately.

How to Use ⇊
  1. Insert the gutta-percha cone into the target calibration hole.
  2. Select the desired ISO diameter.
  3. Trim the extending tip cleanly on the apparatus.
  4. Verify tug-back in the canal and proceed with obturation.

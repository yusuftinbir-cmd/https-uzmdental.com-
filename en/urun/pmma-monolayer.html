Product Details & Technical Specifications

UZM Dental Monolayer CAD/CAM PMMA Disc is produced on UZM Dental's automated production line from cross-linked polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA), ensuring homogeneous density, low shrinkage, and burr-free CAD/CAM milling.

Reliable performance for daily CAD/CAM provisional restorations. Homogeneous PMMA disc structure with clean milling, low shrinkage, and high dimensional stability in A1, A2, and BL3 (Aesthetic White) shades. Available in VITA A1, A2, and Bleach BL3 shades across 16 mm, 18 mm, 20 mm, and 30 mm thicknesses for 98 mm, 95 mm, and Amann Girrbach (AG) milling systems.

Key Features ⇊
Homogeneous Cross-Linked Polymer

Industrial polymerization prevents burring or melting on rotary burs during high-speed milling.

High Dimensional Stability

Low shrinkage rate ensures accurate marginal fit for single crowns and long-span provisional bridges.

Fast Polishing & Plaque Resistance

Dense non-porous surface polishes rapidly to a natural gloss in A1, A2, and BL3 shades.

Certified Biocompatibility

Low residual monomer content, CE & ISO 13485 certified, and registered with the Turkish MoH UTS.

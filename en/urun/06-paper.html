Product Details & Technical Specifications

UZM Dental ISO .06 Taper Absorbent Paper Points are manufactured from pure, high-capillarity medical cellulose fibers in partnership with SANI Medical under ISO 6877 and ISO 13485:2016 quality standards.

Designed for .06 taper rotary canal preparations, effectively removing moisture and irrigation solutions to ensure dry root canals before obturation. Free of chemical binders and loose surface lint, each point maintains its structural rigidity when wet to ensure complete apical drying prior to root canal obturation.

Key Features ⇊
Rapid Capillary Absorption

100% long-fiber medical cellulose absorbs residual irrigants and exudate from the apical third within seconds.

Wet Dimensional Stability

Maintains cone form without fraying, unravelling, or leaving fibers inside the prepared canal.

Exact Taper & ISO Coding

Color-coded crowns match hand and rotary NiTi file tapers for effortless chairside selection.

Hygienic Slide Box

Compartmentalized slide-drawer cassette protects points from moisture and tip deformation.

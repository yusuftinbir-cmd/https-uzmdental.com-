Understanding Monolayer, Multilayer, Clear & X-Ray CAD/CAM PMMA Discs

Comparing monochromatic, multilayer shade-gradient, crystal-clear splint, and radiopaque X-Ray PMMA discs for digital dental workflows.

Polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA) CAD/CAM discs are essential for long-term provisional restorations, smile-design mock-ups, occlusal splints, and implant healing verification. Choosing the correct PMMA formulation improves both clinical function and patient acceptance:

Monolayer PMMA Discs for Everyday Provisionals

Cross-linked Monolayer PMMA discs provide homogeneous density, low residual monomer, and high flexural stability in shades A1, A2, and Bleach BL3 (98×16/18/20/30 mm, 95×18 mm, AG×18 mm) for single crowns and multi-unit temporary bridges.

Multilayer PMMA Discs for Aesthetic Anterior Try-Ins

Multilayer PMMA discs feature a seamless cervical-to-incisal shade transition that mimics natural dentine and translucent enamel—ideal for anterior provisional restorations, gingival emergence conditioning, and smile-design rehearsals.

Clear Splint & X-Ray Radiopaque PMMA

Clear PMMA offers bubble-free optical transparency and wear resistance for bruxism nightguards and occlusal splints, while Opaque / X-Ray PMMA (98 × 18 mm, Shade A1) enables clear radiographic verification of provisional crown seating and cement margins on periapical and CBCT scans.
